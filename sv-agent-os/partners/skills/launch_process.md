# Skill: Launch Process (Phase A–D)

**Use this when:** a candidate has accepted the offer and needs to be launched,
or when checking where an existing partner sits in the timeline.

**Done looks like:** the session's checklist is fully completed with the new
business owner present, the homework is sent, and the next session is on the
calendar. A session half-done because of a computer problem is not done — it
gets rescheduled.

**Source:** the Yager Group / InterNET Services *Qualification Process*
document, Four-Phase Launch Timeline.

## The four phases

| Phase | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A — Business Launch** | Six sessions, ~90 min each, completed over 14 days. Registration plus membership in the mentor-sponsored education program. |
| **B — Building Success Standards** | Developing entrepreneurial and leadership mindset, attitude, and actions; Team Standards and the 30-Day Core Run. |
| **C — Building Your Asset With Results** | Matching personal activity to the team's Success Standards; full commitment to daily/weekly/monthly/yearly success habits. |
| **D — Reaching Points of Recognition** | Continued commitment to success standards, with volume and structure as the major recognition points. Recognition is earned by helping others complete the Team Success Standards. |

Reviewing this timeline with the mentee is itself the point: it clarifies that
there's a reliable plan behind the process.

# PHASE A — Business Launch

Six sessions, ~90 minutes each, over 14 days. **All sessions are done together**
(new business owner + sponsor/coach) on a computer, in person or screen sharing.
If a computer problem comes up, reschedule — that access is critical.

## Session 1 — Company Registration and Communication

1. Introduce the Qualification Process Orientation document. Review dreams and
   goals. Review the Names List (goal: 250+).
2. Register with Amway Global (amway.com). Everyone notes the new Business Owner
   number.

**Amway registration notes:** briefly review all four recommended themed product
stacks with samples, select one, and place the order. Give the samples to the new
Business Owner. Download the Amway app.

**Team communication:** confirm and calendar the next team meetings (weekly),
Business Development Seminar (monthly), and Weekend Conference (quarterly).
Download the team accountability app — access is granted by email submission
including name, coach, upline Emerald and Diamond, IBO number, registration date,
email, and phone. Schedule Session 2.

**Sponsor homework:** email the Product Use Survey and the 30-Day Core Run
Tracker.

**New business owner homework:** review the #10 lifestyle daily; review the
Orientation document; complete the Product Use Survey; add desired products to
the Amway cart; keep building the written 250+ Names List including social media
— **don't contact anyone yet** — segmented **P** (partner candidates) and **C**
(customers); listen to the audios gifted by the coach; keep reading.

## Session 2 — Education Foundation

1. Revisit their #10 lifestyle in detail.
2. Ask: "What have you learned so far as a result of mentorship?"

**Mentorship education registration:** register with IBOCity (ibocity.com) —
needs sponsor, Platinum, and new IBO numbers. Sign the Yager Approved Provider
consent contract (the link redirects to Amway → Contracts → Yager → approve, then
return to IBOCity). Download complimentary audios and explain the complimentary
notification and agreement. Tour the site. Introduce the Amway/Yager onboarding
education for next session. Register/secure sponsored tickets for the next team
meetings, seminar, and weekend conference. Download the IBOCity app and log in;
show how to reach audios on the phone and recommend specific ones. Download and
set up the accountability app.

**Also:** reserve the hotel for the next quarterly conference (card required; may
incur the first night's charge). Confirm the Amway app works. Kick off 30-Day
Core Run daily tracking — first four (blue) columns only — and explain the value
of habit building. Schedule Session 3.

**New business owner homework:** review dreams and goals daily; keep building the
Names List; keep reading and listening; complete the Wellness and Product
surveys; gather images for a Dream Board; choose a profile photo for the My Shop
landing page; practice Make Aware and Door to Mentorship.

## Session 3 — Store and Customer Foundation

1. Review dreams and goals.
2. Review the completed Product Survey and educate on Amway's major product
   lines as you go — Seed to Supplement traceability, Nutrilite farming
   practices, etc. Add all survey product choices to the cart (not to buy — to
   budget and plan the year and create the shopping list and DITTO foundation).
   Review the products in the ordered product stack.
3. **Set up DITTO** — begins the 1st of the following month. Review the Strong
   Start Program: **150 PV with 60% VCS, 12 months out of 12**.
4. **Set up the My Shop store** — note the generated URL (customizable), add the
   profile photo, favorite products, and Wellness Recommender link.
5. **Review Yager onboarding.** KEY STEP: **$300 in customer sales is required
   before they can sponsor team members.** (Amway.com → Education → Yager Group
   Onboarding.)
6. Review the Names List and identify non-candidates as customers.
7. Verify registration for the next team meetings. Optionally schedule a Grand
   Opening within 30 days of launch. Schedule Session 4.

**Sponsor homework:** share the customer conversation scripts/invites (see
`connecting.md`).

**New business owner homework:** review dreams and goals; **use the products**;
continue Core Run tracking; start Week 1 of Yager Onboarding on IBOCity; keep
building toward 250+ names.

## Session 4 — Retail Development

1. Review Verified Customer Sales (VCS).
2. Review how to create receipts.
3. Review the strategy to create **100/50 = 150 PV**.
4. Review the Strong Start Program and their commitment to making it happen.
5. Determine a retail strategy and review the matching scripts/invites: sample
   marketing · Grand Opening · Product & Wellness surveys.
6. Schedule Session 5.

**Sponsor homework:** email the Guide to Business Connections PDF for the next
session.

**New business owner homework:** execute the retail strategy with the
coach/mentor's help; select their top 10 candidates and be ready to explain why
those ten.

## Session 5 — Launch the Network

1. Review goals and dreams.
2. What are you learning — reading, listening, and picking up at team meetings?
3. **The Names List is your business capital.** Review top 10 potential
   candidates (**P**), top 10 potential customers (**C**), and the top 10 closest
   friends and family (the A List) — are they P or C?
4. Develop the story / Make Aware: write it out, practice out loud in front of a
   mirror.
5. Review the Core Run Tracker: number of Conversations, Make Awares, and Drop
   the Messages — huddle on each to improve.
6. Review the Customer and Candidate **2x2s** strategy and the huddle strategy.
   As directed by coach or mentor, reach out to two people, screenshot the text
   response, send it to the coach, then respond with the coach's guidance on
   wording.
7. Schedule Session 6.

**Sponsor homework:** send the QP Master Budget Sheet and the Candidate Tracker
sheet; practice Make Aware and DTM with the coach and/or significant other; reach
out to new candidates/customers as recommended.

**New business owner homework:** buy *The Four Laws of Financial Prosperity*;
conduct 2x2s with huddles as recommended.

## Session 6 — Budget Meeting (~60 minutes)

1. Any questions?
2. Review goals.
3. Review what they're reading and listening to.
4. **Wealth creation vs. debt creation.** Confirm the mindset: we are in the
   wealth creation business, not debt creation. You manage your money or money
   manages you. Education leads to increased income, which can lead to no debt.
   - Live within a budget; track expenses and income for 30–60–90 days.
   - Pay off debt.
   - Hold 6 months of living expenses in liquid funds.
   - Build additional cashflow assets.
5. **Introduce the 4 Laws of Financial Prosperity:** track money for 30 days;
   redirect savings to education (**~$150/month** for first-year mentorship
   sponsored education); acquire and read the recommended books and apply them;
   review the QP Monthly Budget Sheet; decide what to allocate to My Store
   (DITTO); confirm both figures fit the current budget. Budget app suggestions:
   EveryDollar (Ramsey), Daily Budget, Goodbudget, Fudget, NerdWallet.
6. **Establish a separate business checking account** — your business does
   business with Amway. Link Amway's Hyperwallet to it. Customer monies
   (including the owner's own product purchases for the business) get deposited
   there, and business expenses (Amway, education) get paid from it.
7. Review the Business Foundation in the Orientation document: **150 PV with 60%
   VCS**, and reporting VCS receipts of **$300 ($100 × 3 within 6 months)**.

**New business owner homework:** read *The Four Laws of Financial Prosperity*;
track expenses and income for 30 days and review with the mentor; keep tracking
another 30–60 days while implementing the new budget and financial mindset.
