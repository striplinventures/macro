Income disclosure

The figures on this page are a hypothetical illustration of how the Amway compensation plan calculates income at a chosen level of product sales. They are not a promise, guarantee, projection, or estimate of what any person will or is likely to earn, and they are not representative of average results.

They assume every unit shown is actually sold at the stated price every month, that team members perform exactly as entered, that nobody quits, and that all volume qualifies. No costs are subtracted — not product, events, travel, tools, or your own time. None of these assumptions is typical.

Most Amway IBOs earn modest amounts, and many earn nothing. Any conversation in which these numbers are shown must be accompanied by the current Amway Independent Business Owner Compensation Plan / Income Disclosure Statement, which reports actual earnings across all IBOs. Presenting income figures without it is misleading and violates Amway's Rules of Conduct.

Verify before use. This tool ships with placeholder rates. Confirm every price, PV, BV and bonus percentage against current official Amway sources for your market before showing this to anyone.
