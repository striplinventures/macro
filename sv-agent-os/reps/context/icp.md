# Rep ICP — Who Should Build This

## The honest filter

The business asks someone to sell a high-ticket considered purchase, repeatedly, to people
who did not ask. Most people will not do that. Screening hard at the front is kinder than
letting someone spend a year finding out, and it protects your time.

## Strong fit

- **Already sells something**, formally or not — has been paid for persuasion before
- **Has a warm network** they have not burned
- **Owns a real reason** — a specific number attached to a specific goal, not "more money"
- **Coachable** — can be corrected without going quiet
- **Has 8–10 genuine hours/week**, not aspirational hours
- **Long time horizon** — thinks in years, not weeks
- **Cares about the product** independent of the money

## Poor fit

- Needs income *this month* — the timeline does not match and desperation shows in every
  conversation
- Wants to sell without selling
- Cannot afford their own unit
- Has been through several opportunities in quick succession
- Argues with the compensation math instead of asking about it
- Would need to be pushed into every action

## Neutral — do not over-weight

- Prior MLM experience (cuts both ways)
- Big social media following (rarely converts on high-ticket)
- Enthusiasm in the first meeting (regresses fast)

## The question that sorts them

> "What would need to be true, twelve months from now, for you to say this was worth it?"

A specific, personally-owned answer is the signal. Vagueness or an answer aimed at pleasing
you is the opposite.
