# Customer Track — Ideal Customer Profile

## Who this department is for

Retail customers who buy an eSpring. Not partner candidates, not reps — people
who own the product and whose experience with it is the whole relationship.

This department owns everything **after** the sale: the experience, the follow-up,
the social connection, and the referrals that come out of a happy owner.

## Who buys

- Homeowners in PFAS-affected and bad-water markets (Chattanooga, Knoxville,
  Flint, Houston/DFW/San Antonio).
- New-build buyers in the luxury builder communities — River Gorge Ranch, Black
  Creek, Jasper Highlands, Signal Mountain.
- New homeowners arriving through a realtor referral.
- Customers of a rep — a plumber's, builder's, or ADT rep's own customer who
  bought through them.

## What they actually bought

Not a filter. A specific answer to a specific local water problem they were shown
evidence of. That framing carries into every follow-up message: we check on the
water, not on the appliance.

## What a good customer relationship looks like

- Product is installed and in daily use — not sitting in a box.
- They can say out loud what problem it solved for their household.
- They've been asked for a referral **after** a positive experience check, never
  before.
- They're reachable: consented mobile number and email on file.

## Two hard limits

1. **Use restriction.** The Owner's Manual restricts eSpring to residential and
   light commercial (office break rooms). **Hotels and restaurants are
   excluded.** Any commercial placement needs verification with Amway before it's
   pitched. See `../skills/referrals.md`.
2. **Consent.** No automated text or email goes to anyone who hasn't opted in,
   and every message carries a way out. Details in
   `../skills/lifecycle_automation.md`.

## The upgrade path

A customer who keeps referring people, or starts asking how you sell these, is a
**rep** candidate — hand them to `../../reps/`. A customer asking about building
their own business is a **partner** candidate — hand them to `../../partners/`
and run the qualification pattern from the start. Both are one-way; a customer
never hears a business pitch as part of a service follow-up.
