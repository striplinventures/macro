# Rep Track — Ideal Candidate Profile

## Who qualifies

People already in front of homeowners as part of their existing job, who want to
add a product income stream — not necessarily the full business. Plumbers, home
builders, and ADT sales reps are the primary targets.

## Why this profile works

They already have trust and access with the exact homeowner who's the eSpring
buyer. eSpring becomes an add-on to a conversation they're already having — a
plumbing job, a new-build walkthrough, a security-system pitch — not a cold
pitch.

## The offer to them

Add ~$282/unit in profit + bonus to conversations they're already having, with
no change to their primary job. Low commitment to start: no 8-step gate, no
weekly conversation quota. Speed to first sale is the goal, not qualification
depth. Full terms in `../../context/offer_catalog.md`.

## Signals a rep will actually produce

- Already in homes weekly — access is the whole asset.
- Comfortable raising a topic the homeowner didn't ask about.
- Willing to carry the demo stack and run the dip test, not just mention it.
- Comes back within 48 hours with a story about a real conversation.

## Upgrade path

A rep who's consistently selling and asking about building their own thing is a
partner candidate — hand them to the partner track (`../../partners/`) at that
point rather than trying to build both tracks into one pitch.

## Current target

6 active reps in the 60-day sprint. "Active" means they've had a real
conversation and run the demo, not that they've signed up.
