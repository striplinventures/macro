# Partner Track — Ideal Candidate Profile

## Who qualifies

People with entrepreneurial drive who want the full "make money, make friends,
make a difference" model — not just a product income stream. Screen for
character, work ethic, coachability, and integrity over any existing credential
or resume.

## Signals this is a partner candidate, not a rep candidate

- Talks about wanting more freedom and options, not just extra cash.
- Willing to read *Business of the 21st Century* and *The Go-Giver* as a gate —
  and actually engages with them, not just agrees to.
- Has the time and energy for the weekly cadence (25–30 conversations/week), not
  just a side sale here and there.
- Responds well to the "survival vs. winning" framing: side income covers
  survival, team-building is what leads to winning — freedom, travel, options.

## Disqualifying signals (route to `../../reps/` instead)

- Wants the money without the pattern; pushes to skip steps 1–3.
- Says yes to the reading gate and never opens the books.
- Can't or won't commit to a weekly conversation rhythm.
- Only interested in the product, not the model.

None of these are permanent judgments. A rep who grows into the drive becomes a
partner candidate later — that's the intended path, not a consolation prize.

## Where these candidates come from

1. Warm network first (5 Yager list style).
2. Upgrades from the rep track — a rep who's performing and asking "how do I
   build my own thing" is already partner-qualified in practice.

## Current target

2 launched partners in the 60-day sprint. Low volume by design: the pattern
duplicates, so quality at the front end compounds and volume at the front end
does not.
