- Attend the Business Owners' Meeting Night.

- DITTO auto-ship set up for the first week of the month.

**Quarterly**

- Attend the 2–3 day major training event (may include travel and
  accommodation).

- Strong Start Program: 150 PV with 60% VCS, 12 months out of 12.

  sponsor team members.**

  staying on track. The team uses an accountability app for communication between
  teams, groups, and individuals.
