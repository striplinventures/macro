The departments

Customers / Reps / Partners

Same company, three different shapes. One owns the relationship after the sale, one is speed to a first sale, one is a selection process that duplicates itself.

/partners/

Business partnership

Who
Entrepreneurial candidates who want the whole model, not a product income stream.
Gate
The full Yager qualification pattern, start to finish.
Screen
Reliability · Trustworthiness · Willingness to learn · Persistence · Hunger
Shape
Low volume, high retention, duplicates itself.
Posture
We are the selectors, not the sellers.
Movement runs one way — customer → rep → partner — and only on the person's own signal. A customer who keeps referring is a rep candidate; a rep who asks how to build their own thing is a partner candidate. Nothing moves the other direction, nobody hears two pitches in one conversation, and a service follow-up never carries a business pitch.

Standing rules

How the agent behaves

Assumptions are the enemy

If a script, number, name, or contact isn't in the files, ask. Never invent one — hardest on PV figures and water-quality claims.

Compliance outranks copy

Never lead with Amway. No income claims without full disclosure. Education before product. Engage, don't broadcast.

Never blend the offers

Not in one piece of copy, one conversation plan, or one follow-up sequence.

Define done first

Restate the finish line before starting. A vague goal produces a vague stopping point.

One session per goal

Start fresh for each new objective. The master file re-briefs every time, so nothing is lost by starting over.

Scripts are memorized, not paraphrased

The Yager wording is the wording. Rewriting it into something that sounds better breaks the filter.

Still open

  1. The customer lifecycle is designed, not built. Four decisions block automation: which platform sends it, where customer records live, who answers replies, and whether rep-sold customers get their rep's follow-up or a central one.

  3. Referral incentive undecided. Any reward has to clear Amway's customer-incentive rules and the no-income-claims rule. Until then, asks are made on the water story alone, with nothing promised.
  4. about_me.md is a stub. Six story questions are waiting on answers. Until they're filled in, story-driven copy gets flagged rather than invented.
