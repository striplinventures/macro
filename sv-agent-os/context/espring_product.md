## Why eSpring is the right product to build on

- **High ticket, high PV.** One unit carries the volume that many smaller products
  would take to accumulate — which is what makes "ten a month" a meaningful business
  rather than a hobby.
- **Consumable attached.** The replacement filter cartridge creates recurring volume from
  a customer you only sell once. Track this separately; it is the difference between a
  one-time sale and an income base.
- **Demonstrable.** Water quality is something a person can be shown, in their own kitchen,
  with their own water. Few products in the catalog allow that.
- **Universal need.** Everyone drinks water. There is no qualifying question about whether
  the prospect has the problem.

## The PFAS angle

"Forever chemicals" in drinking water is the most effective opener currently available for
this product, because it is a real and widely reported concern rather than a manufactured
one. See `../reps/skills/sales_script.md` for how it is used to open a conversation.

**Constraint:** state only what the official certification documents state. If the unit is
certified to reduce a specific contaminant, say so and cite the certification. If it is
not, do not imply it. Water-treatment claims are regulated, and an overstated claim is both
a compliance problem and the fastest way to lose a technically literate prospect.
