# Skill: Partner Qualification (Yager Qualification Process)

**Use this when:** evaluating, advancing, or writing anything for a partner
candidate. Not for rep-track people — see `../../reps/skills/sales_script.md`.

**Done looks like:** the candidate has moved exactly one meeting forward, the
outcome is logged, and the next meeting has a date on it. "Had a good
conversation" is not a step.

**Source:** the Yager Group / InterNET Services *Qualification Process*
document. Quotes and question wording below are from that document — use them as
written rather than paraphrasing.

## The mindset that governs everything

> We are the **Selectors/Buyers, not the Sellers.**

We are not taking on employees, we are taking on business partners. We are not
looking for financing — we are well capitalized. Every meeting exists to decide
whether this candidate is a good investment of the mentor's time, expertise, and
finances.

We can only help someone who is hungry and is drawn to work with a mentor. If a
candidate has to be convinced, the answer is already no.

## The conversation roadmap

```
Conversations → MA → DTM → MG1 → MG2 → OV1 → FT1 → OV2 → FT2 → Pre-Offer → OFFER → Launch
```

Connecting steps (Conversations, MA, DTM, Call Back) are in
`connecting.md`. Everything after the offer is in `launch_process.md`.

This maps onto the 8-step pattern as: 1. Desires & Goals · 2. Commitment ·
3. List · 4. Connecting/MG1/MG2 · 5. Business Overview (OV1) · 6. Follow
Through/OV2/FT1/FT2/Launch · 7. Review Progress · 8. Rotate Pattern.

Never skip a step to move faster — the pattern **is** the qualification filter.
A candidate who won't sit through the early steps isn't a partner candidate yet;
route them to the rep track (`../../reps/`) instead.

## MEET & GREET 1 (MG1) — Qualifying Introduction

**Mindset:** determine whether this person may be a good fit and a good team
member to invest in.

> **The goal is NOT to give away the book.** The goal is to develop a
> relationship and trust with the candidate.

Open by setting the candidate at ease: the purpose of getting together is to
find out whether there's a trustful relationship here, both directions. Talk
about what learning to be an entrepreneur has been like and what mentorship has
been worth to you.

### Tell your story (~2 minutes)

More detailed than the Make Aware message. Focus on what got you to the point
where you were open to making a change:

- **The Who** — "A while back I was fortunate enough to get introduced to a
  couple of entrepreneurs."
- **The How** — "I found out they are private mentors. Long story short, after a
  few conversations, I was selected for a place in their program."
- **The What** — "Now they are coaching and mentoring me to develop my own
  assets…"
- **The Why** — "…so that I can eventually equal my job income and spend my time
  doing things I want to do."

### Find out THEIR why

1. "Before we get into the mechanics of any business, let's talk about your
   destination, then we can discuss possible vehicles to reach that
   destination."
2. "What would ideal success look like for you — put differently, what is your
   'perfect 10' lifestyle?"
3. Or: "Of these five aspects of the mentorship team, which are most important to
   you?" — **Mentorship · Lifestyle · Wealth Creation · Fun · Making a
   Difference**
4. "What is your current plan to achieve your #10 lifestyle?" Get as much detail
   as possible.

### Cash Flow Quadrants

Lay them out and explain the difference:

- **Left side** — Employed & Self-Employed, sells time for money. E: you have a
  job. S: you own a job.
- **Right side** — Business Owner & Investor, develops ongoing income. B: you own
  a system. I: money works for you.
- Mentors work with mentees to move their thinking and income from the left side
  to the right side.

### The 8 qualifying questions

This is a conversation, not an interrogation.

1. **Are you a finisher?** Do you stay in the race until the finish line? Give me
   an example of a time you resisted the urge to quit.
2. **Would you describe yourself as an outcome-focused person?**
   - If yes: "Tell me something about your life that demonstrates being outcome
     focused."
   - If no: "Would you be willing to learn?" Are you willing to begin with a goal
     in mind — would you shovel horse manure two hours a day for five years to be
     young, wealthy and free, with a #10 lifestyle? Is your #10 lifestyle
     important to you?
3. **Are you willing to be a student and accept mentorship?**
4. **Are you willing to adapt to a different way of thinking** and become a
   student of the entrepreneurial mindset of the right-hand side of the Cashflow
   Quadrants?
5. **Are you willing to dedicate consistent time** to developing your assets with
   cash flow?
6. **Are you interested in helping other people?**
7. **Are you influenced by the opinions of friends or strangers on the internet?**
8. **Do you know someone, or have you personally had experience with Network
   Marketing?**

   If **yes**, follow up:
   - What was their experience?
   - Was the end result to build an asset with cash flow?
   - Was the opportunity focused mostly on selling products and/or recruiting?
   - Did they promote mentorship and coaching?
   - Are they willing to forget everything they know from previous experience
     and from the opinions of others?
   - Then explain: we use a Network Marketing platform to apply mentorship and
     entrepreneurial concepts. Other network marketing concepts focus mainly on
     recruiting and selling — that's "small business" Network Marketing, an S
     Quadrant business. The platform we use is, as far as we're aware, the only
     compensation plan that pays on unlimited width and depth (unlimited, stair
     step, breakaway). That lends itself to a B Quadrant business with a level
     playing field and unlimited income potential. It's big business Network
     Marketing.

   If **no**, mention it's surprising considering it's a $200 billion industry —
   most people have had some exposure to this type of platform.

Then: **"What value would you put on us getting back together?"**

### The book — only if trust has developed

If you and the candidate are connecting and trust is developing, you **may**
introduce *The Business of the 21st Century* by Robert Kiyosaki. Explain it gives
an overview of the project and the team's concepts and mindsets, and that reading
it is required to go any further.

- "You don't have to agree with everything in the book — this is an overview of
  what's available with this mentorship and entrepreneurship."
- Ask: "How long will it take you to read the book cover-to-cover?" If they say
  more than a week, explain that what matters as much as the information is how
  we see them prioritizing what we give them.
- Ask again if needed: "If you were to prioritize reading the book, when do you
  think you can read it by?" Give them 2–4 days.
- Draw their attention to the eight assets in the table of contents.
- Tell them to dress to respect the business opportunity being discussed
  (business attire).

**Next step:** set the time and date for MG2.

## MEET & GREET 2 (MG2) — Book Review

**Mindset:** confirm the candidate has specific goals; is not going to reach them
on their current options (listen); is hungry enough to have followed instruction;
is open-minded about being their own boss; understands why we use the network
marketing model; and is willing to learn how to build it.

1. What are your goals?
2. Did you finish *Business of the 21st Century*?
   > **Do not proceed if they have not finished the book.**
3. What did you think about it?
4. Did you have any challenges with it?
5. What did you learn from it?
6. What concepts did you connect with?
7. Part 3 outlines what to look for in a network marketing concept:
   - Did you understand the eight assets referred to in the book?
   - Which two of the eight stood out to you most?
8. Do you have an interest in the lifestyle that developing debt-free, cashflow
   assets can provide?
9. Any questions or thoughts you'd like to clarify?

**Next steps:** schedule OV1 (Info Session or video conference with the
team/mentor). Loan the candidate *The Go-Giver* by Bob Burg.

## INFO SESSION / OVERVIEW 1 (OV1)

**Mindset:** we are illustrating a business model that is very successful and
offers an affordable option — in time and money — for being more in control of
their lives.

If you run the overview yourself, use the "The Store vs. 'Your' Store"
illustration and ask qualifying questions as you build the example. Keep the
atmosphere relaxed and watch for genuine connection.

- Meeting the team and mentor is about continuing to build trust, not performing.
- We are **not** doing a presentation they pass judgment on. Remind the candidate
  you believe they can do each step.
- Objective: illustrate creating ongoing cash flow from a debt-free asset through
  joint, mutually beneficial effort.
- Tell them the mentorship team provides instruction and mentorship and is
  committed all the way to completion.
- Explain that Amway Corporation provides the products, compensation, contracts,
  guarantees, and administration of rules of conduct.
- Cover the "pyramid" of traditional business.
- Address common issues by personal example: current commitments, time for
  family, "I don't know anyone," "I can't sell."
- Personal Volume consists of customer and personal purchases.
- Getting properly started costs about **$225** for an introductory package of
  products and samples.
- If it makes sense to move forward, meet every 2–3 days.
- Loan a digital audio of your upline Diamond — it expires in 72 hours, and they
  should listen at least once before the next meeting.

**Next steps:** send the Credible Links PDF. Have them start a Names List —
target 100–250 names. Schedule FT1.

**Support materials:** *Why This Idea Will Work* / Meadows – CD214 · *The Magic
of Thinking Big* – BK66 · *The Compound Effect* – BK1178 · Credible Links PDF

## FOLLOW THROUGH 1 (FT1)

**Mindset:** bring their dreams and goals into focus; confirm they're serious
about investing time and effort and open to mentorship. Keep the posture that
you're willing to work with them **if they are serious**.

1. Review their reasons why — goals, expectations, dreams, desires — and put
   rough costs on them. If they want to replace their income and retire, how much
   cash flow does the asset need to produce, and by when? (New car — which one,
   what cost? Kids' education? Travel — where?)
2. Go over the strategy toward those goals, including commitments to:
   - Purchasing from "Their" Store vs. "The Store," using DITTO.
   - Participating in the mentorship education and support system.
   - Understanding the costs covered in the Mentor Sponsored Education Program —
     walk through a budget covering their own shopping, registration, and ongoing
     education (first contract year and beyond).
3. Ask them to explain, in their own words, the difference between the
   Entrepreneurial Mentorship Team and the Amway Corporation.

**Next steps:** send one audio link at a time and ask when they'll finish it.
Schedule OV2 for **after** they'll have listened. Keep building the Names List.

**Support materials:** *An Unusual Opportunity* / Gulick – CED1915

## INFO SESSION / OVERVIEW 2 (OV2)

**Mindset:** be sincerely interested in people's goals and dreams.

Most people do not understand the business plan numbers, compensation plan, and
networking effort the first time around — so go through the business model and
compensation plan **again**. Review "Their" Store vs. "The Store" and DITTO,
review that Personal Volume consists of customer and personal purchases, and
discuss participation in the mentorship program and the education/support system.

**Next steps:** schedule FT2. Send the Event Management PDF.

## FOLLOW THROUGH 2 (FT2)

**Purpose:** help the candidate define what they need to do to reach their #10
lifestyle through this mentorship program and platform. Review mutual
commitments and expectations of a **5-year program of consistent effort**.

### Financial commitments

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Seminars | $30/month per person |
| Conventions | $190/quarter per person |
| Audios, books, app, training website | $90/month (incl. shipping, handling, avg. sales tax) |
| New business owner product stack | ~$225 at the first launch meeting |

These education options continue at their own expense after one year from
registration. Financial commitments also include purchasing products from their
own store and business operational expenses. At the end of each major event,
tickets for the next one become available — will they be financially prepared to
book hotel and travel then? Tax advantages of business ownership get reviewed
with the mentor.

Explain that **100/150 PV with 60% VCS** qualifies a new business owner to expand
and to earn all available bonuses and incentives.

### Time commitments

- **12 to 14 hours per week**
- **5 years**
- Use the Event Management PDF (Team App → Business Resources → Forms) to help
  them design their schedule. Then ask directly: "Can you arrange your current
  time commitments and any potential conflicts to allow for 12 to 14 hours per
  week to pursue your #10 lifestyle?"

### Core training commitments (30-Day Core Run)

1. Read from a mentor-recommended book, 15 minutes minimum per day.
2. Listen to one audio minimum per day.
3. Communicate on the accountability app daily.
4. Attend weekly/monthly/quarterly team meetings, in person where possible.
5. Huddle with the mentor twice weekly minimum.
6. Accountability with coach — review all Team Standards on the Core Run Tracker.

Cover dates of upcoming team meetings, seminars, and conventions.

The candidate reviews everything; if they're a couple, they talk about it
together, to be sure they're ready for a 5-year commitment. They notify the coach
or mentor within 24 hours if they'd like to receive an offer.

> **Even if the candidate says they are "ready," they should call back the next
> day.**

**Next steps:** email the Pre-Offer Questions once they report they're ready, with
a 72-hour expectation for emailed answers. Schedule time with the mentor to
review those answers. Names List goal moves to 250+.

**Support materials:** *You'll Change Your Life When You Change Your Mind* /
Stovall – CD943 · Event Management PDF

## PRE-OFFER MEETING (Question Review)

Ensures the mentor/coach has given — and the candidate understands — the basic
qualifications and activities for getting started.

The mentor reviews the candidate's Pre-Offer Question answers with them. Toward
the end, if everyone is ready, **an offer of mentorship is made**. This is a
celebration of progressing through the selection process: the team has confidence
in the mentee's desires, commitment, and willingness to be mentored.

> Mentorship is **not** required for registration with Amway Corporation. What
> the candidate has qualified and been selected for is participation in our
> mentorship education program.

Offer language: "We're confident you have some good goals and that we can provide
you guidance to accomplish them. As such, we are making you an offer for
mentorship and developing your network marketing team in conjunction with an
Amway business."

**What they bring to the registration appointment:**

1. Computer with internet access (able to screen-share if not in person)
2. Access to the email that will be used for business communication
3. A password they'll use for 3 websites (10 characters)
4. ~$225 for the new business owner product stack
5. A credit or debit card for that purchase

**Next steps:** grow the Names List to 250+. Schedule the Launch Meetings — six
sessions, ~90 minutes each, ideally across two weeks (see `launch_process.md`).
Email them the list of items to bring and the Qualification Process Orientation
document.

## Objection handling — "I need to think about it"

1. Reframe as helping them think it through, not pressuring them.
2. Ask what specifically they need to think about.
3. If the answer stays vague after that, treat it as a likely soft no. Move on;
   don't chase.

This is consistent with the Selector mindset: never push past a real no, help
them get to a clear one. For a "not now," compliment them and follow up in six
months.

## Mentor's team-building sequence

Get to MG1 → get qualified for launch → grow the team one relationship at a time
from there. Team incentive trips are the model once a front-line team of ~5
exists (past: lakefront property; future target: Puerto Rico).

## Logging

After every real candidate conversation, append to `../../memory.md` under
"Partner track": the candidate, the meeting they're on now, what landed, what
stalled. Log after, never before.
