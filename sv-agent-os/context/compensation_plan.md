> **Source:** Amway *Money & Rewards Reference Guide*, marked **"For IBO use
> only. Not for use with prospects."** Treat every number on this page that way —
> it is internal reference for planning and for FT2/OV2 conversations with
> qualified candidates, never marketing material.
>
> **Required income disclosure:** for calendar year 2024, the average income from
> Amway for all U.S. registered IBOs at the Founders Platinum level and below was
> **$723 before expenses**. See amway.com/income-disclosure. Earnings depend on
> many factors including customer base, business experience, effort, dedication,

## Key concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **PV (Point Value)** | Unit amount assigned to each product. Total PV (monthly sales volume + pass-up group volume) determines your Performance Bonus bracket, up to 25%. |
| **BV (Business Volume)** | Dollar figure assigned to each product. Total BV × your PB bracket percent = gross Performance Bonus. |
| **BV:PV ratio** | Weighted average in use as of **September 1, 2025**: 1 PV = **3.43 BV**. |
| **IBO Fiscal Year (FY)** | September 1 – August 31. Also called the Performance Year (PY). |
| **Upline** | The IBO who sponsors you, and the sponsors above them. |
| **Frontline** | IBOs you personally sponsor. |
| **Downline** | Everyone below you in the Line of Sponsorship. |
| **Leg** | Formed when you register someone and help them create a downline group. |
| **Width** | Number of personally sponsored frontlines. |
| **Depth** | Number of IBOs in the downline for each frontline. |
| **Group Volume** | Personal Volume plus pass-up volume from in-market, non-qualified, non-25% legs. Excludes qualifiers at 25% that month. |
| **Ruby / Side Volume** | Ruby PV/BV outside any qualified 25% legs — personal volume plus pass-up from North American legs not at 25% that month. Volume from qualified downline Platinums is excluded regardless of their 25% status. |

Personally sponsoring new legs is the key to generating Side Volume, which
matters for Leadership and Ruby bonuses and several Core Plus incentives.

## Rule 4.12 — customer sales requirement

To earn a bonus on **full BV** for the month, **70% of personal volume must come
from customer sales** (personal and family use does not count toward the 70%),
with a **minimum 60% from Verified Customer Sales (VCS)**. Automated tracking
began September 2022. If total monthly personal volume doesn't meet the
requirement, **BV is prorated accordingly**.

**VCS** is a sale to a customer through an Amway channel — the Amway app, Amway
website, Amway Business Center, MyShop digital storefronts — or a sale from an
IBO's inventory recorded with the Create a Receipt tool. The sale must capture
the customer's name and unique mobile phone number, plus the product and retail
price.

## Baseline requirements

To earn discretionary incentives and non-cash awards such as events:

- Average **150 Personal PV** each month (**1,800 annually**)
- At least **60% of Personal PV** qualifying as VCS
- Signed Amway IBO Registration Agreement on file
- In compliance with IBO Rules of Conduct

## Qualification months

| Qualification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Silver Producer / Qualified Month (Q)** | 7,500 Group PV, **or** 2,500 Group PV over a qualified 25% leg, **or** two qualified 25% legs in the same month. |
| **Personal Qualification (PQ)** | 7,500 Ruby PV **or** 4,000 Ruby PV over any 25% qualified leg. If a downline qualified Platinum doesn't qualify at 25% that month, the upline needs 7,500 Ruby PV that month to earn a PQ. Used for PGGI and Achievers eligibility. |
| **Frontline Qualification (FQ)** | Earned for each frontline IBO who hits 25% Performance Bonus in the month. Max 12 per leg per Performance Year (one per leg per month). Used for FGI eligibility. |

## Core Plan vs. Core Plus

**Core Plan** is the foundational way to earn. **Core Plus** incentives are
discretionary and stack on top. Rough order of earning (may vary):

Retail Margin → Customer Sales Incentive → Personal Performance Bonus →
Differential Bonus → Strong Start Incentive → Bronze Foundation Incentive →
Bronze Builder Incentive → Performance Plus & Elite → Two-Time Cash Incentives →
Monthly Leadership Bonus → Personal Group Growth Incentive → Ruby Bonus →
Frontline Growth Incentive → Monthly Depth Bonus → additional leader bonuses.

# Building to Bronze

## Retail Margin

Selling at retail price keeps the **10% difference** between retail price and IBO
cost. Example: $478 in customer sales earns $47.80 margin.

## Personal Performance Bonus

Total PV determines the bonus level; total BV is multiplied by that percent. Rule
4.12 applies — 70% of personal volume from customer sales, minimum 60% VCS.

**Example — IBO at 3% Performance Bonus level:**

| | PV | BV (3.43:1) |
|---|---|---|
| Personal use | 30 | 102.90 |
| VCS | 120 | 411.60 |
| **Total** | **150** | **514.50** |

Performance Bonus = 3% of $514.50 = **$15.44**

## Customer Sales Incentive (CSI)

Core Plus discretionary incentive rewarding IBOs **at or below the 9% PB level**
with up to 10% of BV on VCS. Available in the year of registration and the
following two performance years.

CSI = (10% − your PB percentage) × BV from VCS. Combined with the 10% retail
margin, that's **20% on VCS**. Capped at **$75 per month**.

| Monthly Performance Bonus | Customer Sales Incentive |
|---|---|
| 0% | 10% |
| 3% | 7% |
| 6% | 4% |
| 9% | 1% |

Total reward = 10% of BV.

**Same example continued:** CSI = 7% of $411.60 = $28.81. **Total earnings
$44.25.**

## Differential Bonus

On top of the Performance Bonus on personal volume, Amway pays on the
**difference** between your PB percentage and that of the IBOs you personally
sponsor, calculated separately for each downline.

Eligibility: IBOs below Platinum must have at least **50 PV from VCS** **or** make
at least one such sale to **10 different customers** (Rule 4.13).

**Example — IBO at 18% Performance Bonus level:**

| Downline | PV | BV | Differential | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 900 | 3,087.00 | 18% − 9% = 9% | $277.83 |
| 2 | 370 | 1,269.10 | 18% − 6% = 12% | $152.29 |
| 3 | 360 | 1,234.80 | 12% | $148.18 |
| 4 | 360 | 1,234.80 | 12% | $148.18 |
| 5 | 360 | 1,234.80 | 12% | $148.18 |
| **Total** | **2,350** | | | **$874.65** |

## Strong Start Incentive (SSI)

Only for new IBOs who signed their IBO contract **in or after June 2024**.

Start the reward period during the registration month or the two months
following by hitting both baselines in a single month:

- **150 Personal PV or more**
- **60% or more of Personal PV qualified as VCS**

Hit both baselines again without missing any months to earn all three rewards.
Each payment can be earned one time. **No longer eligible once a month is missed
after month two.**

| Milestone | Reward |
|---|---|
| After first 3 months | $100 |
| After first 6 months | $300 |
| After first 12 months | $600 |
| **Total** | **$1,000** |

## Bronze Foundation Incentive (BFI)

Eligibility: must have been at or below the **12% PB level in the prior
Performance Year**.

Earn a **30% multiplier on monthly Performance Bonus** by achieving all of the
following in the same month:

- Reaching **9% or higher** on the Performance Bonus schedule
- Helping **at least three legs** you personally or foster sponsored each earn a
  minimum **3% Performance Bonus**
- Meeting baseline: at least **150 Personal PV with 60%+ from VCS** that month

Earnable up to **12 times within 18 consecutive months**, beginning the first
month you earn it — at any level, until the 18 months elapse or 12 payments are
made.

**Example structure:** you at 60 personal use + 90 VCS, three legs at 150 PV each
(3%), PB level 9%. Personal 150 PV / 514.50 BV; VCS 90 PV / 308.70 BV; Group 600
PV / 2,058 BV.

| | 1 month | 12 months |
|---|---|---|
| Performance Bonus | $138.92 | $1,667.04 |
| Customer Sales Incentive (1% of VCS BV) | $3.10 | $37.20 |
| Bronze Foundation (30% of Perf Bonus) | $41.67 | $500.04 |
| **Total** | **$183.69** | **$2,204.28** |

**Consistency rewards** (qualifying months don't need to be consecutive, and can
land any time in the eligibility period):

| Milestone | Reward |
|---|---|
| After earning 3 times | $100 |
| After earning 6 times | $300 |
| After earning 12 times | $600 |
| **Total** | **$1,000** |

## Bronze Builder Incentive (BBI)

Eligibility: your highest qualification on or after September 2019 and prior to
your first Bronze Builder month must be **below Gold Producer**.

Earn a **40% multiplier on monthly Performance Bonus** by achieving all of the
following in the same month:

- Reaching **18% or higher** on the Performance Bonus schedule
- Helping **three legs** you personally or foster sponsored each earn a minimum
  **6% Performance Bonus**
- Meeting baseline: at least **150 Personal PV with 60%+ from VCS** that month

Earnable up to **12 times within 18 consecutive months**, same structure as BFI.

**Example structure:** you at 80 personal use + 120 VCS; legs at 1,000 (12%),
1,000 (12%), 300 (6%); PB level 18%. Personal 200 PV / 686 BV; VCS 120 PV /
411.50 BV; Group 2,500 PV / 8,575 BV.

| | 1 month | 12 months |
|---|---|---|
| Performance Bonus | $658.56 | $7,902.72 |
| Bronze Builder (40% of Perf Bonus) | $263.42 | $3,161.04 |
| **Total** | **$921.98** | **$11,063.76** |

**Bronze Pin:** Core Plus discretionary pin awarded for the first month you earn
the Bronze Builder Incentive. First step toward Silver, Gold, Platinum and
beyond.

> **2× — double up on Bronze incentives.** You can earn the Bronze Foundation
> Incentive and then continue building and earn the Bronze Builder Incentive
> during the same 18-month BFI eligibility period — **two multipliers on your
> monthly Performance Bonus.**

# Aiming Higher

## Performance Plus & Performance Elite

Available to Silver Producer level and above. Baseline: 150 Personal PV with 60%+
from VCS in the month. Available every month you qualify, no payment limit. If
you qualify for more than one multiplier, the highest applies.

| Incentive | Ruby PV | Monthly reward |
|---|---|---|
| Performance Plus | 10,000–12,499.99 | 2% multiplier on Ruby BV |
| Performance Elite | 12,500+ | 4% multiplier on Ruby BV |

## Ruby Bonus

Generate at least **15,000 Ruby PV in one month** for a Ruby Bonus equal to **2%
of Ruby BV** for that month. Part of the Core Plan — all IBOs are eligible.

## Personal Group Growth Incentive (PGGI)

Available to Platinum and above; must qualify Platinum with six Silver Producer
months in the Performance Year. **No Volume Equivalency option.** Increase total
PQ months versus the prior PY, or achieve all 12. Annual baselines: 1,800 PPV and
60% VCS.

| PQs vs. prior year | Annual multiplier | | PQs finished with | Fixed amount (PY26) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +1 to 2 | 20% | | 6–11 PQs | $5,000 |
| +3 to 5 | 30% | | 12 PQs | $10,000 |
| +6 or more | 40% | | 12 PQs w/ 90,000 Ruby PV annually | $15,000 |
| Reach 12 PQs | 40% | | | |

The multiplier applies to Performance Bonus in PQ months (personal and
differential bonuses). Qualifying IBOs earn **the higher of the two** — multiplier
or fixed amount.

## Two-Time Cash Incentives (TTCI)

Reach a new qualification level, then requalify the second consecutive year.
Baseline: FYTD monthly average of at least 150 Personal PV with 60%+ from VCS.
Multiple payments possible for multiple qualifications in a Performance Year, and
second-year payments from a prior qualification can stack on new first-year
payments for higher qualifications.

| Level | 1st year | Consecutive 2nd year |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | $1,500 | $3,500 |
| Founders Platinum | $2,500 | $7,500 |

**Qualification details:**

- **Platinum**, first time: at least six qualified Silver Producer months within a
  12-month rolling period, of which three must be consecutive. Requalification:
  six qualified Silver Producer months within the IBO Performance Year.
- **Founders Platinum:** 12 qualified Silver Producer months within the
  Performance Year. Volume Equivalency qualification by having 10 or 11 Silver
  Producer months with either 90,000 Group PV or 108,000 Total Downline PV is
  acceptable.

## Leadership Bonus

Monthly, through the Core Plan, if you qualify at the **25% Performance Bonus
level** and generate additional group volume. Computed from the bottom up, based
on downline performance, accounting for how much of the bonus rolls up to your
sponsor. With full Ruby Side Volume you keep all of the amount passed up; an IBO
with less than 2,500 PV keeps none of the bonus passed up to them.

## Frontline Growth Incentive (FGI)

Earned by helping downline leaders reach Founders Platinum. Earn an FQ for each
frontline IBO who achieves 25% Performance Bonus level in the month; increase or
maintain your total FQs each year. Max 12 FQs per leg per Performance Year.

Requirements: qualify Founders Platinum by the end of the Performance Year; meet
annual baselines of 1,800 PPV and 60% VCS; meet **48,000 Ruby PV annual total**.

| FQs vs. prior year | Annual multiplier on total Leadership and Foster Bonuses |
|---|---|
| +1 to 2 | 25% |
| +3 to 5 | 50% |
| +6 or more | 75% |

A leg counts as 12 FQs if any of the following: the frontline has 12 months at
25%; the frontline has 10–11 months at 25% plus 108,000 Total Downline PV; or the
leg contains a qualified Founders Platinum. That Founders Platinum may qualify
via Volume Equivalency with 10 or 11 Silver Producer months and either 90,000
Group PV or 108,000 Total Downline PV.

# Event qualification

**Baseline requirements for events:**

- Average **150 or more Personal PV** per month with at least **60% from VCS**
- For annual trips: minimum **1,800 Personal PV** and at least **60% VCS** total
  for the Performance Year
- All award qualifications completed and approved in the FY, per the Business
  Reference Guide
- In compliance with the IBO Rules of Conduct
- IBO must be from the North America market

**New Platinum Conference (NPC):** meet all event baselines; be a first-time
qualified Platinum who hasn't previously attended; in the fiscal year of
first-time Platinum qualification, have at least six Silver Producer months (not
necessarily consecutive) with at least three legs at 6% or higher Performance
Bonus in those same six months. IBOs who don't meet NPC requirements in the FY
they first achieve Platinum can meet the criteria the following year. IBOs unable
to attend the first NPC after qualifying may be invited to a second conference.

**Achievers:** meet all event baselines, and either earn **12 PQ months** or
qualify as an ANA Emerald Bonus Recipient (EBR).

**Achievers Orlando 2026 promotion (PY26 only):** IBOs who don't earn 12 PQs can
qualify with all of: annual baselines (1,800 PV and 60% VCS total); **10 or 11
PQs**; **and 95,000 Ruby PV for the year**. Qualified EBRs in North America still
get an invitation if they meet annual baselines.

## How this maps to the current position

Dems is at ~6% Performance Bonus (~460 PV/mo, 79% VCS, 0 sponsored legs) — see
`business_info.md`. Reading that against this plan:

- VCS at 79% clears both the Rule 4.12 minimum (60%) and the baseline
  requirement, so BV isn't being prorated.
- At 6%, the CSI is worth 4% of VCS BV — and CSI eligibility runs only through
  the registration year plus the following two performance years, so it's a
  closing window given the 3/17/2022 registration date.
- **Bronze Foundation Incentive** is the near-term target and it needs three
  things at once: 9%+ PB (600+ PV), three personally or foster sponsored legs
  each at 3%+ (100+ PV each), and 150+ Personal PV with 60%+ VCS. The "sponsor 3
  legs" goal in `business_info.md` is this requirement.
- **Bronze Builder** raises the same shape to 18% PB (2,500+ PV) with three legs
  at 6%+ (300+ PV each) — and can be earned inside the same 18-month window as
  BFI for two stacked multipliers.
- Differential Bonus below Platinum requires 50 PV from VCS or one sale to each of
  10 different customers, so customer breadth matters, not just volume.

Confirm current-month figures against the Amway app or Business Center before
using any of this in planning — these are structural rules, not live numbers.
