# Partner ICP — Lighter-Touch Involvement

A **partner** is not a rep. They are not building a team. They refer, host, or plug in at a
level that fits an existing life, and the relationship should be structured so that is a
respectable end state rather than a failed rep.

## Strong fit

- **Sits on trust** in a community — trades, health, real estate, church, neighborhood
- **Talks to homeowners** in the normal course of their work
- **Already bought** and genuinely likes the product
- **Wants a side stream**, not a second career
- **Would not want a team** and says so plainly

## Partner archetypes

| Archetype | Why they work |
|---|---|
| Plumber / contractor | Already in kitchens, already trusted on water |
| Realtor | Talks to people moving into a home they want to improve |
| Health / wellness practitioner | Clients already ask about water |
| Community connector | Volume of warm conversations, low sales pressure |
| Satisfied customer | Lowest friction of all — they already own it |

## Poor fit

- Wants the income of a rep without the activity of one
- Would need managing
- Cannot speak about the product without overstating it *(a real disqualifier — their
  claims become your compliance problem)*

## Partner → rep

Some partners eventually want more. Do not push it. The trigger is **them asking**, twice,
unprompted. Then run the rep ICP properly rather than assuming a good partner is a good rep.
