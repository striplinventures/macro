# Skill: A-List Customer Conversations (My Shop)

**Use this when:** opening the store to close friends and family, or building the
customer side of the Names List.

**Done looks like:** a real appointment on the calendar — a face-to-face or video
tour of the My Shop site that ends with them registered as a customer and an order
placed. "They said they'd check it out" is not done.

**Source:** the Yager Group / InterNET Services *Qualification Process* document,
"Guide to Create Your Business Connections."

## Who to start with

Your **A-List** — close friends and family who are **not** potential mentorship
candidates. These are the people who, if you opened a brick-and-mortar store,
would drive across town just to shop with you and support your new business.

Segmenting the Names List into **P** (partner candidates) and **C** (customers) is
what this depends on — see `../../partners/skills/connecting.md`.

## The conversation

> "Hello ______. I am super excited that… / I wanted to share with you that… I've
> just launched my own online store. We have a number of best-in-class, exclusive
> brands in the areas of Sports Nutrition, Energy, Supplementation, clean Skin
> Care, Beauty and Personal Care, as well as environmentally conscious Home Care."
>
> "Would you consider giving me the opportunity of earning your business?"

Then:

1. Set a face-to-face or video appointment.
2. Tour the My Shop website together.
3. Register them as a customer.
4. Place a product order with them.

## After the first order

Once the customer relationship exists, the **Wellness Survey** and **Product
Survey** expand the level of service you can offer — they turn a one-off order
into an ongoing relationship and give you a real reason to follow up.

## Two things this feeds

- **VCS.** These are customer sales, and they count toward the 60% VCS
  requirement and the $300 ($100 × 3 in six months) needed before you can sponsor
  anyone. See `../../context/compensation_plan.md`.
- **The follow-up sequence.** Capture name, mobile, email, and consent at the
  point of sale — `lifecycle_automation.md`.

## Note on scope

This is the broad Amway customer conversation, covering all product lines. The
eSpring-specific conversation — PFAS opener, dip test, segment pitch — is a
different script, in `../../reps/skills/sales_script.md`. An A-List customer who
turns out to have a water problem gets that conversation, not this one.
