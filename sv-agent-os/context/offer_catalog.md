# Offer Catalog

Two offers. Two audiences. They are never presented together.

## Offer 1 — eSpring (product sale)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Retail price | $1,299 |
| IBO cost | $1,169.10 |
| PV | 340.85 |
| BV | $1,169.10 |
| Profit + bonus per unit at scale | ~$282 |
| Financing talking point | PayPal, ~$44/month |

**Who it's for:** homeowners in PFAS-affected and bad-water markets; new-build
buyers; realtor and builder referrals.

**Use restriction:** residential + light commercial (office break rooms) only.
Hotels and restaurants are excluded per the Owner's Manual. Verify with Amway
before any B2B/commercial push.

**Sold through:** direct sale, or through a rep (Offer 2).

## Offer 2 — Rep track (sell eSpring inside your existing job)

**Who it's for:** people already standing in homeowners' houses as part of their
day job — plumbers, home builders, ADT sales reps.

**The offer:** add ~$282 per unit in profit + bonus to conversations they're
already having. No change to their primary job, no 8-step gate, no weekly
conversation quota.

**What they get to start:** demo stack (eSpring water bottles + dip test kits),
the PFAS script, the segment list, and a 48-hour first follow-up. Full
onboarding steps in `../reps/skills/sales_script.md`.

**Success metric:** speed to first sale, not qualification depth.

## Offer 3 — Partner track (build the business)

**Who it's for:** entrepreneurial candidates who want the full "make money, make
friends, make a difference" model, not just a product income stream.

**The offer:** the complete Yager business-building pattern with mentorship,
team building, and the incentive-trip structure — screened for character, work
ethic, coachability, and integrity.

**The gate:** the full 8-step qualification pattern. The gate *is* the offer's
quality control — see `../partners/skills/qualification_process.md`.

**Success metric:** partners who duplicate the pattern themselves, not partners
who buy.

## How the offers connect

- Offer 1 sells to a homeowner. Offer 2 recruits the person who reaches that
  homeowner. Offer 3 builds the person who builds the team.
- The only upgrade path is **Offer 2 → Offer 3**: a rep who is consistently
  selling and starts asking how to build their own thing gets handed to the
  partner track. Nothing moves the other direction, and no candidate hears both
  pitches in the same conversation.
- Compliance note for all three: no income claims without full disclosure. The
  ~$282/unit figure is a per-unit economics fact for people being onboarded, not
  an earnings promise for marketing copy.
