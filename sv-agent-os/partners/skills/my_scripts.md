# Skill: My Scripts (Dems)

The Yager blanks, filled in. Structures live in `connecting.md` and
`qualification_process.md` — these are my words.

**Mentorship first, always.** The mentor relationship is the story. Not the water
business, not sales, not how I got here. Those come later, and only if asked.

**The Make Aware is the first part of the MG1 story.** Same opening, then it keeps
going. Learn the short one; the long one is the short one plus what came before.

> **Draft.** Review with the mentor, then memorize. Practice out loud.
> Source: `../../context/about_me.md`.

---

## 1 · Make Aware — 20 to 30 seconds

**People I meet.** Answers "What do you do?"

> "A while back I got connected to a couple of entrepreneurs. Turned out they're
> private mentors — long story short, I chased them down and got selected to work
> with them. Now they're coaching me on building income-producing assets, so I
> can eventually replace my job income and spend my time doing what I want."

**People I know.**

> "I didn't want to say anything until I got accepted, but I got connected to a
> private business mentor and had to chase him down before he took me on. He's
> coaching me on building assets. I'm learning a lot — I think it's going to make
> a huge difference in my life."

## 2 · "What is it?"

One answer, then stop.

> "We're learning how to develop financial assets of our own." / "Assets with cash
> flow."

If they push: *"Honestly, I'm still a student myself."* · *"That's a 45-minute
conversation over coffee."* · *"That'd be a great question for my mentor."*

## 3 · Door to Mentorship

Only after I've found their why.

> "From what you've said, getting ahead matters to you. If I could get you
> connected to my mentor, what kind of value would that be to you?"

If it lands: *"I'll be talking to my mentors soon. Text me your number and I'll
let you know what I find out. What's one thing about yourself you'd want them to
know?"*

If not: compliment, six-month list, "let's stay in touch."

## 4 · Call back

> "My mentor asked a couple of things about you I didn't know how to answer. He
> suggested I meet with you. Want to grab coffee and go from there?"

## 5 · MG1 — the two-minute story

Open with the Make Aware above, word for word. Then keep going:

**What made me open to it.** "Before that I was in sales at LeafGuard, and what I
found was a glass ceiling — you can only go so far. I'd already dropped out of
community college because I couldn't see how business school was going to get me
into business."

**Why I chased him.** "He and his wife walked away from their jobs about forty
years ago, at thirty-one and thirty. Since then they've had the freedom to
travel, mentor people, and spend their time on what they actually care about.
That's what I'd always pictured for myself, so I wasn't going to let it pass."

**What I actually got.** "I thought it'd be a rocket ship — millionaire overnight.
What I got was growth, and a lot of telling myself no. Travel I didn't take, cars
I didn't buy. The mentorship is the part I'd never give up. It's like Rocky —
I've got Mickey in my corner. Somebody who's already been in the fight and can
coach me through it."

**Then hand it to them.** *"Before we get into the mechanics of any business,
let's talk about your destination — then we can talk about vehicles to get you
there."*

## 6 · The water story — only if asked

MG1 isn't about the business. Keep it to three sentences.

> "Carpet companies near me were dumping chemicals into local water so the carpet
> would come out waterproof, and nobody told people what it was doing to them.
> *Erin Brockovich* is a real thing here. I saw a need in my own community and
> realized I could fill it."

## Compliance

- Never lead with Amway.
- My mentor's story is a story. Nothing implies a typical or expected result, and
  no income claims without full disclosure.
- The Porsches-and-travel line is what I **denied** myself. Reversed, it becomes
  an income claim.
