# Skill: eSpring Sales Script (NEPQ + PFAS Opener)

**Use this when:** writing or running an eSpring conversation, or onboarding a
rep to run one. Not for partner candidates — see
`../../partners/skills/qualification_process.md`.

**Done looks like:** the prospect has answered questions about their own water
situation, seen the dip test, and either booked a next step or given a clear no.
A delivered monologue is not done.

## Framework

NEPQ-based — question-led, not pitch-led. PFAS contamination is the primary
door-opener: lead with local water-quality facts, let the prospect draw the
conclusion, and let the product show up as the answer to a problem they just
described themselves.

## PFAS door-opener facts (local, verified)

- Dalton/Shaw and Calhoun/Mohawk carpet mills, Conasauga River watershed, 2024
  lawsuits, Erin Brockovich investigation.
- Tennessee American Water issued a November 2024 warning to 40,000 Chattanooga
  customers; TTHMs measured up to 89.1 ppb.

Use these exactly as written. Don't round the numbers, don't extrapolate to
health outcomes, and don't add facts that aren't on this list — if a prospect
asks something beyond it, say you'll get the answer and come back.

## Segment the pitch by who's in front of you

| Segment | Angle |
|---|---|
| Custom home builders | Spec-line upgrade |
| Realtors | Closing-gift referral channel |
| Landlords / property managers | Portfolio deal |
| Investors / flippers | Resale value-add |
| Churches, gyms, small offices | Single decision-maker, whole-building |
| Plumbers | Install/referral partner — not a buyer |

Commercial limits apply: residential and light commercial (office break rooms)
only. Hotels and restaurants are excluded. Verify with Amway before pitching
anything else commercial.

## Close mechanics

- **Demo stack:** eSpring water bottles + dip test kits. Build and carry these
  before anyone goes out — the missing demo stack was the #1 gap identified on
  the first D2D run.
- **Affordability:** PayPal financing at ~$44/month. Lead with the monthly
  figure only after the problem is established, never as the opener.
- **Numbers:** $1,299 retail. Full economics in
  `../../context/offer_catalog.md`.

## Objection handling

Same core reframe as the partner track: don't push past a real no, help them
think it through, ask what specifically is holding them back. Then answer that
one thing rather than restarting the pitch.

## Rep onboarding checklist (get them to first sale fast)

1. Hand them the demo stack and this script.
2. Walk the PFAS story once, live, so they can repeat it in their own words.
3. Give them the segment table above and help them map it to who's already in
   their own customer base.
4. First follow-up at 48 hours: did they have a conversation yet, and what
   happened.
5. Log the result in `../../memory.md` under "Rep track" — which opener they
   used and how it landed.

## Compliance

Education first, product second. No income claims without full disclosure. No
independent branding of Amway products. Never lead with "Amway." Video over
static, engage rather than broadcast. Full rules in
`../../context/business_info.md`.
